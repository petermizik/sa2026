    ☕🥨 Cape Town Roasteries, Markets & Local Eats

    ☕ Specialty Coffee Roasteries (Batch Brew & Flat White)
    • Rosetta Roastery (101 Bree St & Woodstock) — #1 third-wave micro-roastery for single-origin filter batch brew, V60 & flat whites (Day 13).
    • Origin Coffee Roasting (28 Hudson St, De Waterkant) — Pioneer of SA specialty coffee; warehouse roastery with filter bar & flat whites (Day 1).
    • Truth Coffee Roasting (36 Buitenkant St) — Mark Wiens' 8 AM pick; iconic steampunk roastery with house-roasted single origins (Day 2).
    • Espressolab Microroasters (The Old Biscuit Mill) — Nordic light-roast specialty coffee bar right inside the Biscuit Mill (Day 13).
    • Terbodore Coffee Roasters (Franschhoek, Day 11) & Olympia Café (Kalk Bay, Day 13) & Loading Bay / The Stranger's Club / Jason Bakery / Kanéla Café (Days 2 & 14).
    🧺 Local Markets & Franschhoek Wine Tram
    • Oranjezicht City Farm Market (OZCF — Granger Bay): Open Wednesday Night (17:00–21:00 — Day 2!) AND Saturday & Sunday mornings (Day 13!) right by the ocean.
    • Neighbourgoods Market at The Old Biscuit Mill (Woodstock): Open Saturday & Sunday mornings (Day 13!) — local street food, specialty coffee & designers.
    • Hop-On Hop-Off Franschhoek Wine Tram (Day 12, Sat 5 Dec): R370/pp open-air double-decker tram across Boschendal, Babylonstoren, Grande Provence & Rickety Bridge.
    🥪 Iconic Local Food, Sarmies & Restaurants
    • Mariam's Kitchen (St George's Mall — Mark Wiens 9 AM pick): Authentic Cape Malay Steak Salomie & Chicken Salomie (roti curry wrap) or Gatsby (Day 2).
    • Kalky's (Kalk Bay Harbour — Mark Wiens 2 PM pick): Legendary harbourside Hake & Slap Chips, Peri-Peri Prawns & Snoek right on the pier (Day 13).
    • Pappa's Deli (137 Waterkant St, De Waterkant — @eats_of_ally): Takeaway hatch deli sarmies (brekkie bun, banh mi, hot fried chicken — Day 1 & 14).
    • Lucky Lou's (158 Kloof St — @eats_of_ally): Buzzy bar/hangout famous for the OF Burger & chips (Day 12 Saturday night).
    • Nelson's Eye (9 Hof St, Gardens — Mark Wiens 7 PM pick): Classic steakhouse for Kudu Carpaccio & 500g Springbok Steak (Day 1).
    🌅 5 Local Rules & Hidden Gems (@visit.capetown__)
    • 1. Table Mountain Timing: Avoid midday! Go late afternoon (15:30 on Day 1) or early morning, and pre-book Cableway tickets online.
    • 2. Quieter Beaches & Tidal Pools: Visit Llandudno & Noordhoek, and don't miss Miller's Point Tidal Pool (ocean rock slide & kelp forest — Day 13!).
    • 3. Slow Peninsula + Tintswalo Atlantic: Dedicate a full slow day to the Peninsula (Day 13) and book sunset cocktails at Tintswalo Atlantic.
    • 4. Slow Wine Estates: Pick 3–4 estates on the Franschhoek Wine Tram (Day 12) and visit Constantia Glen Winery (5 mins from Kirstenbosch — Day 14).
    • 5. Chapman's Peak & Twelve Apostles: Drive Chapman's Peak at sunset (Day 13) and stop for an ocean cocktail at The Twelve Apostles Hotel (Day 2).
